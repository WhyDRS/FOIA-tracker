Freedom of Information Act Request

U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission
Office of FOIA Services

Re: DTC Tokenization Services - quarterly report packages under the December 11, 2025 no-action letter

Dear FOIA Officer:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 USC 552, I request the quarterly report packages that The Depository Trust Company ("DTC") has provided to staff of the Division of Trading and Markets pursuant to representation 10 in Section III(b) of DTC's December 11, 2025 request for no-action relief concerning the Preliminary Base Version of the DTCC Tokenization Services.

Reference:
https://www.sec.gov/files/tm/no-action/dtc-nal-121125.pdf

Please provide each responsive quarterly report package in the SEC's possession through the date the SEC performs its search, including attachments, exhibits, schedules, appendices, transmittal materials, and any corrected, amended, revised, or supplemental versions.

Representation 10 states that each package contains information for the covered calendar quarter concerning:

1. the identities of Participants participating in the Preliminary Base Version as of quarter-end;
2. the total number of shares and total value of security entitlements tokenized pursuant to a Tokenization Instruction during the quarter;
3. the total number of shares and total value of security entitlements maintained as Tokenized Entitlements as of quarter-end;
4. the average daily volume of Tokenized Entitlements transferred between Participants during the quarter;
5. the count of CUSIPs that were de-tokenized during the quarter;
6. the list of securities eligible to be Subject Securities as of quarter-end;
7. the number of Registered Wallets at quarter-end;
8. the names of blockchains on which Tokens were recorded during the quarter;
9. the names of blockchains DTC determined not to approve for inclusion in the Preliminary Base Version during the quarter and the reason for each determination;
10. the names of approved blockchains that DTC knew to be unavailable during the quarter for the service;
11. instances in which DTC used its "root wallet" to address Conditions Requiring Reversal, other than to service a corporate action event, during the quarter; and
12. disclosures made to Participants under representation 13 during the quarter.

Please also provide any DTC fee or charge information for the Preliminary Base Version that was included in, attached to, or transmitted with a quarterly report package or otherwise supplied to Division staff with that package.

For each package, please include the transmittal email or other record sufficient to identify the date received by the SEC and the quarter to which the package relates. I do not seek duplicative copies of the same package merely because multiple SEC custodians received it.

If production of the complete packages would materially delay processing, please treat this request in the alternative as a request for an existing spreadsheet, database export, table, or other structured record containing the representation 10 fields. In particular, please do not omit the quarter-end total number and total value of security entitlements maintained as Tokenized Entitlements.

Please produce responsive electronic records in their native or readily reproducible electronic format where available. For spreadsheets, tables, databases, or structured exports, please provide the native machine-readable format rather than converting the data only to PDF. For email, please provide .eml where reasonably practicable, preserving available headers, MIME structure, attachments, and the relationship between each message and its attachments.

If any portion of a responsive record is withheld, please release all reasonably segregable non-exempt portions and identify the FOIA exemption relied upon for each withholding. Where a record contains both participant-specific material and aggregate statistics, please segregate and release the aggregate statistics even if participant-identifying information is withheld.

WhyDRS requests classification as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and requests a waiver of search, review, and duplication fees under 5 USC 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR 200.80(g)(12). The records concern the SEC's oversight of a systemically important clearing agency's tokenization service and the operation of relief from ordinary clearing-agency, SRO rule-filing, and systems-compliance requirements. WhyDRS intends to use the records for public research, source preservation, editorial analysis, and unrestricted public dissemination. Disclosure would materially improve public understanding of the scale, use, infrastructure, reversals, outages, and regulatory oversight of the service. The request is not being made for a commercial use.

If the fee waiver is denied or fees otherwise may exceed $100, please notify me before incurring additional chargeable fees.
