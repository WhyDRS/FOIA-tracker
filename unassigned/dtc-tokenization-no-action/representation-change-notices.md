Freedom of Information Act Request

U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission
Office of FOIA Services

Re: DTC Tokenization Services - notices of changes to no-action representations

Dear FOIA Officer:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 USC 552, I request records concerning changes to the representations on which the Division of Trading and Markets' December 11, 2025 no-action position for DTC's Preliminary Base Version of the DTCC Tokenization Services is based.

Reference:
https://www.sec.gov/files/tm/no-action/dtc-nal-121125.pdf

At the conclusion of Section III(b) of DTC's request, DTC represented that, before implementing any change to representations 1 through 16, it would notify Division staff of its intent to change the representation and, if necessary, re-engage with Division staff regarding proposed updates to the relief.

Please provide responsive records through the date the SEC performs its search, including:

1. each notice from DTC to Division staff stating an intent to change, amend, replace, suspend, or cease following any of representations 1 through 16;
2. any revised representation, redline, comparison, description of change, implementation plan, or attachment transmitted with such a notice;
3. any final SEC or Division response, acknowledgement, condition, concurrence, non-concurrence, modification of relief, or other final communication addressing such a proposed change; and
4. records sufficient to identify any re-engagement between DTC and Division staff concerning whether a proposed change required updated or additional no-action relief.

Please limit the search to records tied to the representation-change commitment described above. I do not seek general policy discussions about tokenization that are unrelated to an actual or proposed change to the representations governing this no-action relief.

Please produce responsive electronic records in their native or readily reproducible electronic format where available. For email, please provide .eml where reasonably practicable, preserving available headers, MIME structure, attachments, and the relationship between each message and its attachments.

If any portion of a responsive record is withheld, please release all reasonably segregable non-exempt portions and identify the FOIA exemption relied upon for each withholding. To the extent deliberative material is withheld, please release the factual description of the proposed change and any final agency position.

WhyDRS requests classification as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and requests a waiver of search, review, and duplication fees under 5 USC 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR 200.80(g)(12). These records concern how the SEC administers and modifies a significant no-action arrangement governing DTC's tokenization service outside the ordinary SRO rule-change process. WhyDRS intends to use the records for public research, source preservation, editorial analysis, and unrestricted public dissemination. The request is not being made for a commercial use.

If the fee waiver is denied or fees otherwise may exceed $100, please notify me before incurring additional chargeable fees.
