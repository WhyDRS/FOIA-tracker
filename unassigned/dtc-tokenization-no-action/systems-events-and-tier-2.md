Freedom of Information Act Request

U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission
Office of FOIA Services

Re: DTC Tokenization Services - Systems Event and Tier 2 records under the December 11, 2025 no-action letter

Dear FOIA Officer:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 USC 552, I request records in the possession of the Securities and Exchange Commission concerning "Systems Events" and the Tier 2-or-better system rating commitments for the Preliminary Base Version of the DTCC Tokenization Services described in DTC's December 11, 2025 request for no-action relief.

Reference:
https://www.sec.gov/files/tm/no-action/dtc-nal-121125.pdf

Please provide responsive records through the date the SEC performs its search in the following categories:

1. Each initial notification DTC provided to Division of Trading and Markets staff under representation 9 after DTC identified a disruption, compliance issue, or intrusion that materially impacted, or was reasonably likely to materially impact, operation of the Preliminary Base Version and therefore constituted a "Systems Event."
2. Each update DTC provided to Division staff while working to resolve a Systems Event.
3. Each notification DTC provided to Division staff stating that a Systems Event had been resolved.
4. Attachments or factual incident materials transmitted with those notices or updates, including incident summaries, timelines, root-cause descriptions, impact assessments, remediation descriptions, or post-incident reports, to the extent such records were provided to or maintained by the SEC.
5. Final or factual records in the SEC's possession sufficient to show the Tier 2-or-better system rating assigned to LedgerScan and Factory, including any final assessment, exception, downgrade, change in rating, or record supplied to the SEC concerning the four-hour recovery-time objective, two-minute maximum data-loss objective, primary/secondary-location capability, or annual out-of-region disaster-recovery and resumption testing described in the no-action request.
6. Records sufficient to identify any incident involving LedgerScan, Factory, or another new system used for the key functions of the Preliminary Base Version that DTC discussed with Division staff for the purpose of determining whether the incident met the Systems Event threshold, regardless of the final determination.

For item 6, I am not requesting a broad search for every internal SEC discussion about system risk. I seek records sufficient to identify the incident, the date, the system affected, and whether DTC or Division staff treated it as a Systems Event. If deliberative portions are withheld, please release reasonably segregable factual portions and any final determination.

Please exclude duplicate copies of Systems Event material already contained in a quarterly report package if the identical record is produced in response to a separate request for those packages.

Please produce responsive electronic records in their native or readily reproducible electronic format where available. For email, please provide .eml where reasonably practicable, preserving available headers, MIME structure, attachments, and the relationship between each message and its attachments.

If no records responsive to items 1 through 3 are located, please state that no responsive Systems Event notices were located rather than creating a new substantive record.

If any portion of a responsive record is withheld, please release all reasonably segregable non-exempt portions and identify the FOIA exemption relied upon for each withholding.

WhyDRS requests classification as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and requests a waiver of search, review, and duplication fees under 5 USC 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR 200.80(g)(12). These records concern the SEC's oversight of operational resilience and incident reporting for DTC's tokenization service while the service operates under no-action relief from Regulation SCI. WhyDRS intends to use the records for public research, source preservation, editorial analysis, and unrestricted public dissemination. The request is not being made for a commercial use.

If the fee waiver is denied or fees otherwise may exceed $100, please notify me before incurring additional chargeable fees.
