Freedom of Information Act Request

U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission
Office of FOIA Services

Re: DTC Tokenization Services - Division staff data requests under representation 16

Dear FOIA Officer:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 USC 552, I request records concerning Division of Trading and Markets requests for information or data from DTC under representation 16 of DTC's December 11, 2025 request for no-action relief concerning the Preliminary Base Version of the DTCC Tokenization Services.

Reference:
https://www.sec.gov/files/tm/no-action/dtc-nal-121125.pdf

Representation 16 states that DTC would make information and data maintained in its systems available to Division staff upon request.

Please provide responsive records through the date the SEC performs its search consisting of:

1. records sufficient to identify each request by Division staff to DTC for information or data under representation 16, including the date, subject, and categories of information or data requested;
2. the responsive information or data DTC provided to Division staff in response to each such request, including attachments and structured data files; and
3. any final or factual transmittal record sufficient to associate each DTC response with the corresponding Division request.

Please exclude routine quarterly report packages required by representation 10 when those packages were supplied without a separate representation 16 request. If a Division request sought additional detail, underlying data, or a supplemental dataset relating to a quarterly package, that additional request and response remain within scope.

Where responsive data exist in a spreadsheet, CSV, database export, JSON, or other machine-readable format, please produce the native structured file rather than converting it only to PDF. For email, please provide .eml where reasonably practicable, preserving available headers, MIME structure, attachments, and the relationship between each message and its attachments.

If any portion of a responsive record is withheld, please release all reasonably segregable non-exempt portions and identify the FOIA exemption relied upon for each withholding. If participant-identifying or other granular information is withheld, please segregate and release non-exempt aggregate data, field names, dates, and metadata sufficient to understand the scope of the information DTC provided.

WhyDRS requests classification as a representative of the news media under 17 CFR 200.80(g)(2)(vi) and requests a waiver of search, review, and duplication fees under 5 USC 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 17 CFR 200.80(g)(12). These records concern the SEC's ongoing supervision of DTC's tokenization pilot under no-action relief and would illuminate what information the Division considered necessary to monitor the service beyond the prescribed quarterly package. WhyDRS intends to use the records for public research, source preservation, editorial analysis, and unrestricted public dissemination. The request is not being made for a commercial use.

If the fee waiver is denied or fees otherwise may exceed $100, please notify me before incurring additional chargeable fees.
